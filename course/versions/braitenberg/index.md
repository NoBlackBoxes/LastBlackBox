**LBB-100** is the shortest LBB course: a four-session path to building a [Braitenberg Vehicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braitenberg_vehicle), a simple robot brain that senses its environment and responds with action. You will assemble **NB3** with no black boxes, from analog electronics up through a microcontroller-driven robot.

## Sessions

### Session 1 - [Analog Electronics](sessions/01_analog-electronics/README.md)
- Atoms, electrons, and sensors. Build a circuit and read a voltage.

### Session 2 - [Magnets and Semiconductors](sessions/02_magnets-and-semiconductors/README.md)
- Magnets, motors, and transistors. Make something move.

### Session 3 - [Digital Computers](sessions/03_digital-computers/README.md)
- Data, logic, memory, and computers. Program a microcontroller.

### Session 4 - [Robot Control](sessions/04_robot-control/README.md)
- Control and robotics. Wire it all into a Braitenberg vehicle.

## The curriculum

Braitenberg traces four boxes — sensors, motors, transistors, and computers — into a working sense-and-respond robot. Hover any box to highlight it; click to open its folder on GitHub for the deeper material.
