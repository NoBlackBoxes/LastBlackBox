# LBB: Build a Brain

<p align="center">

</p>

**LBB-101** walks you through building a brain — and the body around it — from raw electronics up to a robot that sees, hears, and learns. Over eight sessions you will assemble **NB3** from atoms onward, with no black boxes.

## Sessions

### Session 1 - [Sensing the World](sessions/01_sensing-the-world/README.md)
- Atoms, electrons, sensors, light. **Project**: *Build a light sensor.*

### Session 2 - [Making Things Move](sessions/02_making-things-move/README.md)
- Magnets and motors. **Project**: *Build a speaker.*

### Session 3 - [Digital Decisions](sessions/03_digital-decisions/README.md)
- Transistors, amplifiers, circuits, and power. **Project**: *Build a light-sensitive motor.*

### Session 4 - [How Computers Work](sessions/04_how-computers-work/README.md)
- Data, logic, memory, computers, and FPGAs. **Project**: *Program a microcontroller.*

### Session 5 - [Build a Robot](sessions/05_build-a-robot/README.md)
- Control and robotics. **Project**: *Build a Braitenberg vehicle.*

### Session 6 - [The Software Stack](sessions/06_the-software-stack/README.md)
- Linux and Python. **Project**: *Connect wirelessly and play with Python.*

### Session 7 - [How the Internet Works](sessions/07_how-the-internet-works/README.md)
- Websites, servers, networks, and security. **Project**: *Build a remote-control robot.*

### Session 8 - [Artificial Intelligence](sessions/08_artificial-intelligence/README.md)
- Audio, vision, learning, and intelligence. **Projects**: *voice-activated, vision-enabled NB3.*

## The curriculum
